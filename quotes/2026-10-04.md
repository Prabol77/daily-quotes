> "The sun shines brightest not to erase the shadow, but to expose where it hides. The real illusion isn't the darkness, but believing a clear sky means the storm was never real."

**Category:** Perspective

## Reflection

Peace does not rewrite what happened. A storm can pass and the sky can clear, yet that does not make the storm any less real. Sometimes clarity is simply the courage to acknowledge what was there without allowing it to define what comes next.

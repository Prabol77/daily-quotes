> "A morning of unbroken sunlight does not erase the storm gathering for the afternoon. True strength is not built on the belief that clear skies will last forever, but on holding your center when the elements inevitably clash."

**Category:** Resilience

## Reflection

Favourable conditions are never a guarantee that challenges won't come. Strength grows when we stop depending on everything staying calm and learn to remain grounded when opposing pressures collide.

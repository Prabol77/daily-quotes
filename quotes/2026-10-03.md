# October 3, 2026

> "Light does not defeat the night; it simply makes the dark visible. True calm isn't found beneath a clear sky, but in learning to sit unbothered while the storms pass through you."

**Category:** Inner Peace

## Reflection

Darkness does not always need to be conquered, and storms do not always need to be stopped. Sometimes clarity is simply being able to see what surrounds us without letting it disturb what is within us.

> "Abundant light hides the depth of the horizon just as easily as darkness does. Clear paths do not eliminate the uncharted, they simply make us forget that the unseen remains waiting just beyond our view."

**Category:** Humility

## Reflection

Clarity can make us feel as though we understand the whole picture, but every visible path has its unknowns. Humility means remembering that what we can see is only part of what exists, even when everything appears clear.

> "An open, cloudless sky offers complete clarity, yet reveals nothing of its own depth. The absence of shadow isn't always proof of truth—sometimes, the brightest light simply makes us blind to what lies beneath."

**Category:** Discernment

## Reflection

What appears completely clear may still leave important truths hidden. The absence of visible obstacles does not mean we understand the full picture. Discernment means looking beyond appearances and questioning what brightness or certainty might be concealing.

> "Even the strongest tree grows quietly where no one can see it. Progress is not always the branch reaching toward the light; sometimes it is the root learning to hold through the next storm."

**Category:** Patience

## Reflection

Not every season of growth looks like movement. The unseen work of building patience, discipline, and inner stability matters just as much as visible achievement. When difficult weather arrives, the strength developed quietly is what keeps us standing.

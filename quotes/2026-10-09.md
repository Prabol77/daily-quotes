> "The quietest sunshine carries the silent seed of a storm. True balance isn't built by clinging to the clear sky, but by anchoring yourself so deeply that even sudden lightning cannot shake your ground."

**Category:** Inner Strength

## Reflection

Balance is not the promise of predictable circumstances; it is the stability we build within ourselves. When the weather changes without warning, being deeply rooted helps us face the turbulence without losing our direction.

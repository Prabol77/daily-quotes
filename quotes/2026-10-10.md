> "Unbroken sunlight reveals everything on the surface, but hides the depths in sheer brilliance. True sight isn't measured by how brightly your path is lit, but by whether you can perceive what remains lingering in the shadows."

**Category:** Awareness

## Reflection

What is most visible is not always the whole truth. Brightness can draw our attention to the surface while leaving deeper realities unnoticed. Awareness means looking beyond what is obvious and being willing to recognize what remains unseen.

> "Unbroken clarity can blind us just as deeply as total darkness. When nothing stands in the light, we often forget that depth is only revealed when shadows are allowed to fall."

**Category:** Insight

## Reflection

Clarity is valuable, but it can also make us overlook what remains hidden. Shadows provide contrast and reveal dimensions that constant brightness conceals. Sometimes understanding requires us to accept uncertainty rather than assume that everything visible is everything there is.

> "The quietest waters often reflect the heat most intensely; true strength isn't about remaining untouched by the blaze, but holding your composure while the surface burns."

**Category:** Resilience

## Reflection

Composure is not the absence of pressure. Sometimes the strongest response is to remain steady while everything around you feels chaotic. The surface may burn, but strength is choosing not to let the fire dictate what happens within.
